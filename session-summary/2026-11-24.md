## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Calendar endpoint request body** (it worked, 200, 66 KB for April 2026):
  ```bash
  curl -s -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d '{"calendarStartDate":"2026-04-01","calendarEndDate":"2026-04-30"}' \
    https://pub-edmonton.escribemeetings.com/MeetingsCalendarView.aspx/GetCalendarMeetings
  ```
  - Records sit under `d`, with keys `ID`, `MeetingName`, `StartDate` (`YYYY/MM/DD HH:MM:SS`), `MeetingType` and `MeetingDocumentLink`.
  - `MeetingDocumentLink` is a list of `{Type: Agenda|PostMinutes|Video, Format: .pdf|HTML, Url}`.
  - Agenda HTML is at `/Meeting.aspx?Id=<ID>&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English`, minutes at `&Agenda=PostMinutes`.
  - Attachment links in the agenda HTML are `<a href="...DocumentId=N">title.pdf</a>`.
  - The whole agenda as a PDF is itself a DocumentId (04-15: 294405).
  - **This belongs in `data/DATA.md`** when it is written.
- **"Continuation" meetings** carry no documents; the agenda lives on the parent meeting. A crawler shouldn't flag them as missing data. Record them, but expect no links.
- **Signed bylaws** are attached as "Signed Bylaw NNNNN - Published <date>.pdf" on the meeting that gave third reading.
- Scratch from the lookup (April 2026 calendar JSON; 04-15 agenda and minutes; 04-28 agenda) is in `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-opc-civic-docs-index/7ea4e0ff-17a9-4496-9ece-35c0f68ab9a1/scratchpad/bylaw/`. It's disposable, has no KEEP file, and can be crawler fixture material.
